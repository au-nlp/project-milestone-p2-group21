\section{Datasets}
\subsection{SMOL}
SMOL\cite{caswell2025smolprofessionallytranslatedparallel} (Set of Maximal Leverage) is a high-quality translation corpus from English to more than a hundred low-resource languages. SMOL includes SMOLSent, SMOLDoc and Gatitos\cite{jones-etal-2023-gatitos} which represent sentence-level, document-level and token-level translations respectively.  


\subsection{Monolingual Cantonese corpus by M. Dare, et. al}
In their work on unsupervised Mandarin-Cantonese machine translation, Dare et. al.\cite{dare2023unsupervisedmandarincantonesemachinetranslation} collected a large corpus of monolingual Cantonese sentences from various online sources. In total 912258 lines were collected by scraping and cleaning data from Wikipedia, Instagram, and a few other sites including a few corpusses compiled for previous academic work.
