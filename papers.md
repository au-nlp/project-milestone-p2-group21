# RELEVANT PAPERS!

- SMOL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.12301
- No Language Left Behind: https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.04672
  - Good review of what languages are underrepresented. (Big language list)
  - Also has a dataset
- xComet: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.10482
  - Model for evaluating translation models
- Cantonese papers:
  - CantonMT: https://aclanthology.org/2024.amta-presentations.9.pdf
  - Unsupervised Cantonese <-> mandarin: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.03971
    Uses a common embedding for Cantonese and Mandarin and two decoders, one for each
    Constructs a Cantonese dataset fra Wikipedia, Instagram and Youtube subtitles.
    Still achieves pretty mediocre results, at least on the manually evaluated.
    Dataset available here: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1NdO299X4YGoKdQDrS4wLgYrdCBar3Usp
  - Also this one: https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/21677/21426
